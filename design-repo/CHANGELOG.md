# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (design-review-pending)

Review pass on whole-page wrappers, taxonomy and provenance.

* Sections 96 to 113: nine whole-page wrapper sections (`bespoke.brand`, `bespoke.careers`, `bespoke.change`, `bespoke.climate-leads`, `bespoke.manifesto`, `bespoke.soc-report`, `legal.document`, `story.content-frame`, `customers.all-customers`) were split into 26 per-part sections along measured DOM boundaries; templates 31 and routes 571 are unchanged. Before the split `bespoke.change` was classed hero at 12522px (88.1% of its page), `bespoke.manifesto` 5185px (77.0%), `bespoke.careers` 4732px (73.7%), `bespoke.climate-leads` 7407px (81.5%), `bespoke.brand` 2785px (62.3%), `customers.all-customers` 6587px (60.7%), `legal.document` 3026px (64.2% on `/legal/privacy`).
* New closed `schema/section-contract.schema.json`; every contract now has distinct `source` {id, tag, file, lines, anchor} and `semantic` {id, category} objects (generated by `extraction/generate_provenance.py`, source ranges resolved against the real files by `verify_all.py`). The free-form `category` field was removed.
* `retrievalEligible` (closed boolean, reason of 30+ characters when false) wired through contracts, the closed schema, the allowlist, `semantic_validate.py` (rule `RETRIEVAL_ELIGIBILITY_PINNED`) and `verify_all.py`, pinned independently in each. No cookie, consent, modal, dialog or overlay section exists in the clone, so the pin covers `block.Spacer` and `block.HorizontalRule`.
* Classification: `ONE_HERO` now counts HERO-class sections for every template (17 sections), `SEMANTIC_CATEGORY_CLOSED` added (graph rules 24 to 26); `block.HeaderRoot` is CONTENT (a header band, not a hero) with `HERO_FIRST` openerSections; brand press-kit, summit opener and careers intro reclassified (CONVERSION, HERO, HERO) in the IA; 15 secondary-CTA sections recorded in `extraction/classification-review.json`.
* `extraction/wrapper_gate.py` added to `verify_all.py` with `sectionHeights` (564 routes measured at 1440px by `extraction/measure_section_heights.mjs`) and a reviewed whitelist (3 entries, agent attestation, not human).
* `schema/generate_pagespec_schema.py` regenerates the per-section parts of `pagespec.schema.json`; `verify_all.py` fails on drift.
* Adversarial suite 55 to 102 mutations (32 controls); `prove_drift.py` 45 to 82 cases, optional `--scratch`.
* The root `ia.json`, `IA.md` and `matrix.csv` were updated and regenerated with `build.mjs` (IA sections split or reclassified to match); `validate.mjs` reconciles (571 routes, 64 templates).

## 0.1.0 (initial build, status design-review-pending)

Built from scratch from the clone source, CLONE_SPEC.md, PAGES_SPEC.md, ASSET_MANIFEST.md and IMAGE_CREDITS.md.

* Tokens: 393 tokens across foundation, semantic, component and layout tiers; light theme resolved, dark band documented; LLM catalog, policy and allowlist (version 1.0.0).
* Contracts: 17 primitives, 15 components, 96 sections (12 homepage sections including chrome, 49 block types, shared page sections, bespoke pages).
* Templates: 31 templates partitioned with the project's own classifiers; 571 routes = 564 templated + 7 redirects, each exactly once.
* Compatibility graph: 24 rules with severity and named exceptions, each checked against the real block orders when written; identical rule ids in `schema/semantic_validate.py`.
* Asset roles: 14 closed roles with generation policies; six roles pinned (people, customer logos, brand wordmark, award badges, decorative illustrations, form endpoints).
* Motion: closed pattern vocabulary with honest reduced-motion fallbacks; corrections to early recon numbers recorded in `extraction/measured-values.json` (awards 2400ms, logo strip 1060ms/2016ms, ticker 4500ms, promo 5000ms/46px, nav dropdown fade-scale, product countdown continues under reduced motion).
* Verification: `verify_all.py` (parity, citations, counts, routes, graph, assets, tokens, self-containment), `adversarial_test.py` (55 mutations, 32 controls), `prove_drift.py` (each drift check proven to fail on injected drift).
* Known gaps carried honestly: tablet never measured, 12 of 180 events sampled, three inferred routes, block-library hover nudges and FaqFullWidth not animated in the clone.
